# Printer is not printing

**Priority:** P3.

1. Is the printer on, free of paper jams and showing no error? Is the right printer selected?
2. Clear the print queue on the workstation and print a test page.
3. Check that the printer is on the network: ping it or open its status page.
4. Reinstall or update the driver following the standard procedure.
5. If the printer shows an error code, note it and escalate to the hardware vendor.
